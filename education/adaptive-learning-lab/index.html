2. Assess and identify prerequisites

Administer the printable probes independently if possible. Enter the number correct (0–8) and support level. Four to eight questions are instructional checks, not validated diagnostic scores.

Start with a baseline assessment.

3. Personalized weekly packet

4. Progress monitoring and review

Proposed protocol: skill-specific checks weekly; adjust practice every week; review longitudinal response after approximately six instructional weeks; rescreen broad prerequisites every 8–12 weeks. Do not decide mastery from a single small probe or raw speed alone.

Scope and evidence

This prototype provides arithmetic fluency and targeted prerequisite practice, not a full mathematics curriculum. Pair with grade-appropriate explicit instruction and conceptual problem solving. Supports a limited selection of grade 2–6 Common Core-related skills. Prerequisite connections and cutoffs are transparent engineering heuristics, not psychometrically validated measures. Not a diagnosis of dyscalculia or evidence of grade-level proficiency.
